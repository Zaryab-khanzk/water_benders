What we build

Every piece is drawn for its site, then engineered to run quietly for decades.

Tiered and sculptural fountains

Stone, bronze and steel forms with choreographed jets, from courtyard centrepieces to civic landmarks.

Water walls and cascades

Sheet, ribbon and rain-curtain effects for lobbies, facades and gardens, with acoustics tuned to the space.

Reflecting pools and basins

Still, mirror-finish water with hidden overflow, lighting and filtration built into the architecture.
